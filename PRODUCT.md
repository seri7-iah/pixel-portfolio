# PRODUCT.md

A personal portfolio for POP (IT student, Philippines) that looks and feels like an 8-bit adventure game.

## Audience
Recruiters, clients and classmates. Most arrive with little time and one question: "what has this person built?" A few will stay to explore.

## Goal
Make a memorable first impression, then get out of the way so the work is one click from the top. Everything below the fold rewards scrolling, but nothing essential depends on it.

## The world
One continuous scroll that goes deeper with every section, like descending through a world rather than clicking between pages.

1. **Home**: night sky, stars and grassland. Pix, Gloop and Foxel. This is the first impression and the signpost that jumps straight to any area.
2. **Projects**: a cave of stone, torches and crystals. The work lives here, and each project links directly to its live site.
3. **About**: an Ember world, a fiery underground of maroon rock, lava glow and grey ash. Who POP is, told warmly.
4. **Contact**: a Void world of near-black violet sky and pale cream floating rock. A quiet, unmistakable final invitation.

Going deeper is thematic, not a maze: the signpost and normal page anchors stay available so a recruiter can reach Projects without scrolling the whole world.

## Tone
Playful, friendly, clear. The game layer is decoration; the writing is plain and useful. Cozy at Home, awe in the cave, warmth in Ember, calm in the Void.

## Principles
- Original characters and art only, in a blocky 8-bit style. Never copy Minecraft/Mojang characters, names or lookalikes; original designs with a similar level of chunkiness are fine.
- Text sits directly on the scene. The speech box is the only boxed element; there are no cards or panels.
- Characters and scenery stay light: a small cast per area, animation only while that area is on screen, everything paused when the tab is hidden, and full respect for reduced motion.
- Works at 360px and on desktop, keyboard accessible, with visible focus.
- Content is written later; the structure must hold with placeholder copy.
